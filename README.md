# Kunal Basu

My background is in electronics and communication engineering at the University of Delhi. I am interested in how people and neural networks represent the same things: where they agree, and what each might be missing.

During a Max Planck internship, I worked with Sander van Bree on a replication of Chen and Bonner's *Universal dimensions of visual representation*. The results held up at full scale, including the out-of-distribution tests. I am now interested in a more specific question: which interpretable visual dimensions are shared by humans and models, and which belong mainly to one or the other?

## Selected work

- **[Universal dimensions replication](https://github.com/Kunal-Basu-FOT/universal-dimensions-replication)** — Code, figures and results for the LAION-fMRI replication with Sander van Bree.
- **[ABIDE connectome benchmark](https://github.com/Kunal-Basu-FOT/abide-gnn-benchmark)** — Compared an MLP with five graph neural networks using AAL and CC200 functional connectomes. In the saved runs, the MLP performed better on AUROC.
- **[SFARI EEG preprocessing](https://github.com/Kunal-Basu-FOT/sfari-eeg-preprocessing)** — Recording audit, quality control and task-specific preprocessing for seven EEG paradigms. The downstream prediction study is still to come.
- **[Speech experiments at IISc](https://github.com/Kunal-Basu-FOT/speech-multitask-learning)** — Language, dialect and gender recognition across recording domains, developed during an INSA–NASI–IASc summer fellowship.
- **[Acoustic feature analysis](https://github.com/Kunal-Basu-FOT/XAI-Acoustic-Feature-Analysis)** — Speaker classification from acoustic features, with local LIME explanations.
